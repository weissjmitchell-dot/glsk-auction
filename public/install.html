Add GLSK to your iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari. If you opened it inside another app, copy the link below and paste it into Safari.
  2. Tap Share. Depending on your Safari layout, it may be inside the More or page menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.
  4. Tap the new GLSK icon and sign in with your existing league account.

Can't find Add to Home Screen? Look under Edit Actions in the Share menu. You can also use GLSK directly in your browser.

One league. The same account.

Your contracts, trades, rookie rights and draft history stay connected to the existing league. Installing does not create another team or account.

An internet connection is required for league activity and drafts. The link does not grant team access; contact the commissioner if you need help signing in.
