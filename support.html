How can we help?

Support for GLSK League Office, the companion app for members of the Great Lake State Keepers fantasy football league.

Contact support

For sign-in problems, app issues, feedback or feature requests, contact Mitchell Weiss:

weiss.j.mitchell@gmail.com

Please include your team name, what you were trying to do, and any error message. A screenshot and your app version can help. Never send your password or verification codes.

Trouble signing in?

GLSK is intended for existing league members. Use the email associated with your league account. If you cannot sign in or your team is missing, email support so we can help you check your access.

League and account help

Contact support for questions about contracts, rookie rights, bid dollars, trades, player releases or draft records. You can also email us with privacy questions or to request account deletion.
