GLSK League Office Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

GLSK League Office is operated by Mitchell Weiss for members of the private Great Lake State Keepers fantasy football league. This policy explains how information is handled through our app and website.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your name or display name, email address, account identifier and team association. Authentication is provided through Supabase.
  • League records: player ownership, contracts, rookie rights, fantasy bid balances, draft picks and selections, trades, releases and transaction history.
  • Communications and contributions: league chat, message-board posts and replies, league notes, and other content you submit. Imported league discussions may include author names, message content and timestamps from the league’s Google Group.
  • Activity information: read status for chats and notifications and records of league actions, used to keep the app and league history up to date.
  • Support information: information you send when requesting assistance or contacting us about your account.
  • Technical information: our hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, request information, authentication events and error logs to operate and secure the service.

The app uses local storage to maintain sign-in sessions and preferences. If you enable browser push notifications on the website, we store the subscription endpoint, notification keys and device/browser information needed to deliver them. Browser push registration is disabled in the current native iPhone app.

How information is used

We use information to sign members in, associate accounts with teams, manage league assets and drafts, display communications and history, maintain unread indicators, provide requested notifications, resolve support requests and protect the service.

GLSK does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

Who can access information

League records and content shared with the league are visible to other authorized members according to the app’s access controls. Commissioners have additional administrative access. Please avoid posting information you do not want other league members to see.

Supabase provides authentication and database services, and Vercel hosts the website and server functions. These providers process information needed to operate the service. Browser notification delivery also involves the applicable browser’s push service. Information may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service or its users.

Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while needed to provide access and support. League transactions, drafts and shared discussions may be retained across seasons to preserve league history.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information and account. We may verify your identity before acting. We will review linked league records and remove or anonymize personal information where appropriate while preserving shared league history where necessary. Any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons will be explained in our response. Copies in service-provider backups may remain until those backups expire.

Your choices

You can choose what content to submit, sign out of your account, and revoke browser notification permission through your device or browser settings. To ask about your data or request account deletion, email weiss.j.mitchell@gmail.com. Never send your password or sign-in codes.

Security

We use authentication and access controls to restrict account and league information. No electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

GLSK League Office is intended for the league’s adult members and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate and address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated to league members.
